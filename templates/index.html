How to Use SignAI
Getting Started
  1. Position your hand clearly in the camera view
  2. Make ASL gestures from the phrase library
  3. Hold the gesture steady for best recognition
  4. Nod your head to confirm uncertain translations
Confidence Indicators
  • High Translation is confident
  • Medium Nod to confirm
  • Low Try repositioning or nod to confirm
Tips for Best Results
  • Use good lighting
  • Use a plain background
  • Keep your hand in frame
  • Start with basic phrases
  • Practice the gestures before using
Advanced Features
  • Text-to-speech for audio feedback
  • Export translations for later reference
  • Adjust settings for optimal performance
  • Search the phrase library for specific signs
